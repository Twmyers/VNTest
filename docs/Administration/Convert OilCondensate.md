



# Convert Oil/Condensate

The Oil and Condensate Converter converts oil forecasts and history to
condensate and vice versa. The converted entity’s product list is also
changed from oil to condensate and vice versa.

To convert oil or condensate

1.  Select the entities with oil or condensate forecasts you want to
    convert.
2.  In the **Administration** menu, select **Data Cleanup** \&gt; **Convert
    Oil/Condensate**.
3.  In the Oil & Condensate Converter dialog box, select a conversion
    option.
4.  If required, select **Apply to child entities**.
5.  Click **OK**.
