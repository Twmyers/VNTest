

# Payout Calculation

The payout is calculated by subtracting the capital stream from the
revenue stream and then determining the date when this new stream has a
cumulative value greater than or equal to the total capital spent.

When expressed as a number, it is the number of years (or partial years)
after the first capital is spent that (capital - revenue) exceeds total
capital.

Step revenue = Net revenue

Step capital = WI capital

Total capital = Total WI capital excluding abandonment and salvage
