

# Delay the Economic Limit

On Economics \| General, you can delay
the economic limit for a certain number of months beyond the first
capital spent date or the first production date, whichever occurs first.
This enables you to better handle certain classes of uneconomic
properties, such as wells drilled with overspends or the drilling of
uneconomic wells to preserve acreage. This option is available both at
the well level and in scenario calculation options.
