

# Add a Manual Entity-level Price

You can enter manual prices at the entity level in two ways.



Value
Navigator calculates royalties based on royalty stream prices,
not sales price for some Canadian entities.



## Option 1

Override the prices displayed on **Economics \| Prices & Costs \|
Prices** by typing over the prices in the
Results column. If the Results column is
not displayed, click Expand details below the grid. The values you enter
manually are displayed with a grey background.

## Option 2

Select **Manual** from the **Price Set List** on **Economics \| Prices &
Costs \| Prices** and enter your own prices for each product.

This prices gird on Economics \| Prices & Costs
\| Prices uses sparse data entry. You only need to enter your
price one time and
Value
Navigator will continue to use that price until you enter another
price.
