



# Enter Allowances for Capital and Operating Costs

To enter operating or capital cost allowances

1.  Select the **Economics \| Prices & Costs \| Capital Costs** or
    **Operating Costs**.
2.  Add operating costs or capital costs as required (see
    [Enter Capital Costs](Enter%20Capital%20Costs.md) or
    [Enter Operating Costs](Enter%20Operating%20Costs.md)).

Costs with (GCA) are included in the GCA (gas cost allowance)
calculation.
