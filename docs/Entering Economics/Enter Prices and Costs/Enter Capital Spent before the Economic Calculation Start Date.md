



# Enter Capital Spent before the Economic Calculation Start Date

If unrecovered capital remains prior to the Economic Calculation Start
Date, do one of the following:

- Enter the unrecovered capital amount as the Payout Reversion Value

  OR

- Use the **Economics Detail—Before Tax Expanded** report to determine
  when the capital will be recovered and use that date to [Enter Reversions](../Set%20Up%20Interests%20and%20Royalties/Enter%20Reversions.md)rather than a payout
  reversion.

If there are special contracts in place where a capital amount is used
for a payout reversion but that amount is not entered in **Economics \|
Prices & Costs \| Capital Costs**, enter the amount as the **Payout
Reversion Value**.
