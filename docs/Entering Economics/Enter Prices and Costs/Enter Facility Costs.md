



# Enter Facility Costs

If it is necessary to have a different working interest applied to
facility capital or operating costs, facility capital costs, abandonment
costs, and salvage costs can be entered on **Economics \| Prices & Costs
\| Capital Costs**. Facility operating costs are entered on **Economics
\|Prices & Costs \| Op Costs/Other Revenue**. These costs will be
applied according to the Facility Interest on **Economics \| Interest
and Royalties**. See
[Enter Capital Costs](Enter%20Capital%20Costs.md) and
[Enter Operating Costs](Enter%20Operating%20Costs.md).
