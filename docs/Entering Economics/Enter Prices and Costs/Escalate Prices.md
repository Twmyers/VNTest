



# Escalate Prices

You can escalate a price by indicating an Escalation percentage (annual)
in a price deck or on an entity.

Price escalation only applies to manually entered prices (overrides) and
begins one year after the overridden value.

To escalate prices in a price deck, see [Create a Stream Price](../../Prices%20and%20Currencies/Create%20and%20Edit%20Price%20Decks/Create%20a%20Stream%20Price.md) .

To escalate operating costs, see
[Escalate Operating Costs](Escalate%20Operating%20Costs.md) .

To escalate prices on an entity

1.  On **Economics \| Prices & Costs \| Prices**, click **Expand
    Details** to display the Results columns and the Escalation %
    (annual) row.
2.  In a product’s **Result** column, enter the **Escalation %
    (annual)**.
3.  In the year the escalation is to begin, enter the new price and
    press **Enter**.

The edited cell is displayed with a grey background to indicate a manual
override of the stream price. The escalation of the price begins one
year after the manual override.
