



# Enter Factors

Factors are entered on **Economics \| Interests & Royalties**. Factors
are explained below.

| Factor | Description |
|----|----|
| Pooling Factor | The amount of interest in combined small or irregular tracks of land into a tract in order to meet government regulations for drilling and producing a well (drill spacing unit). |
| Tract Factor | The amount of interest in a tract of land. |



If the factor is not displayed, click the **Interest Properties** list
and select it.
