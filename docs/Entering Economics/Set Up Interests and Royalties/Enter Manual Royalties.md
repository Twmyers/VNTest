



# Enter Manual Royalties

Manual Royalties are entered on **Economics \| Interests & Royalties**.

You can enter manual royalties for a lease. For an American case (when
the country of the case is United States in the **Case Properties**
window), you can also add an Ad Valorem royalty.

To enter a manual royalty for a lease

1.  Select an interest in the **Interests** window.
2.  On the **Interests** tab, confirm your Entity Properties selections
    for the lease. The country you select determines the type of manual
    royalties you can enter.
3.  On the **Royalty Properties** tab, enter the Mineral Owner.
4.  **Select Use Manual Royalties** and click **Edit**.
5.  Enter the royalty percent beside the appropriate date. If you have
    selected an American case, you will have an additional column to
    enter Ad Valorem royalties.
6.  Click **OK**.\
    Manual royalties appear as an Unadjusted Royalty in economics
    reports.



When a manual royalty is applied to an American case, this royalty will
be added to the royalties of any fiscal regimes that are being applied
to the case. To replace the fiscal regime with the manual royalty,
deselect the fiscal regime.
