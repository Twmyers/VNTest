



# Enter a Working Interest

Working interest is entered on **Economics \| Intersts & Royalties**.
You can enter

- **General working interest**: If this is the only interest entered, it
  is applied to the capital and operating costs.
- **Operating costs**: The interest percentage entered is applied to WI
  Operating Costs entered on the Operating Costs tab.
- **Capital costs**: The interest percentage entered is applied to WI
  Capital Costs entered on the Capital Costs tab.
- **Facility Interest**: The interest percentage entered is applied to
  FI Operating Costs and FI capital entered on the Operating Costs and
  Capital Costs tabs, respectively.



If the interest is not displayed, click the **Interest Properties** list
and select it.
