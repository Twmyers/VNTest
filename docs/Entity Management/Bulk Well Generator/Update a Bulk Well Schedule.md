



# Update a Bulk Well Schedule

If you edit the schedule’s source well or the schedule’s definitions on
**Bulk Well Generator\| Definition**, you need to update the schedule on
**Bulk Well Generator \| Timetable**.

On **Bulk Well Generator \| Timetable,** three symbols are used to
indicate what will be updated:

- A red dot (![](../../Images/Update-a-Bulk-Well-Schedule-1.png)) indicates the source well was edited and
  its parameters will be updated.
- A sunburst (![](../../Images/Update-a-Bulk-Well-Schedule-2.png)) indicates a well has been added on the
  **Definition** tab and updating the timetable will add a new well.
- A red X (![](../../Images/Update-a-Bulk-Well-Schedule-3.jpg)) indicates a well has been removed on the
  **Definition** tab and updating the timetable will remove the well.

To update a well schedule

1.  Go to **Bulk Well Generator \| Timetable** and click **Generate
    Schedule**.\
    The Generate Bulk Well Generator dialog box displays the changes
    that will be made.
2.  Click **OK**.
