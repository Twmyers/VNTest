



# Common Termination Entity

A common termination entity (CTE) consists of at least one well and one
facility (cost entity) in the same field. Creating a CTE enables you to
determine the economic limit of the field by ignoring well-level
economic limits and calculating the economics for all the wells and
facilities as one entity.

You can add a CTE to another CTE or Ring Fence Entity by right clicking
the CTE in the explorer and selecting **Add to** \&gt; **CTE** or **Add
to** \&gt; **Ring Fence Entity**.

Creating a CTE involves the three basic steps below. See the following
sections for more details.

1.  [Filter to Wells or Organize the Wells by Field](Filter%20to%20Wells%20or%20Organize%20the%20Wells%20by%20Field.md).
2.  [Create a Cost Entity](Create%20a%20Cost%20Entity.md)(facility) and assigning it costs.
3.  [Create a Common Termination Entity](Create%20a%20Common%20Termination%20Entity.md) .
