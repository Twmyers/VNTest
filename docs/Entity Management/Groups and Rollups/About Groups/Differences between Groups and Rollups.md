



# Differences between Groups and Rollups

Groups are useful for evaluating commingled wells, or when you want to
group wells into a unit. Groups are not ideal for entities that have
incentives or quantity-based royalties, because the averaging effect
could lead to inaccurate results. Rollups are useful for tracking
drilling programs. The differences between groups and rollups are
described below.

| Groups | Rollups |
|----|----|
| Groups are entities and are included in reserves reconciliation. | Rollups are simply lists of wells. and not included in reserves reconciliation. |
| Wells in a group appear in the well list only as a subset of their group parent. | Wells in a rollup still appear in the well list. |
| You can run economics on a group. However you can also see economic results of the child wells although they are overridden at the group level. | You cannot run economics on a rollup. |
| You can edit a group forecast. | You can't edit a forecast on a rollup. |
| The working interest an on-times of the grouped wells are averaged. | Each well’s on-time is reflected at the rollup level. |
| In the Entity Explorer, Groups can be viewed when *Groups* or *Wells and Groups* is selected from the drop-down menu. | In the Entity Explorer, rollups can only be viewed when Rollups is selected from the drop-down menu. |
