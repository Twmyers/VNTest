



# Edit a Group Forecast

When you create a group,
Value
Navigator automatically creates a manual forecast (a summation of
the child forecasts). When a group has a manual summation forecast,
decline parameters do not appear on **Predictions \| Declines** and the
columns containing forecast data on **Predictions \| Data** are grey.

If you want a forecast that can be edited, you can [best fit](../../../Forecasting/Create%20and%20Edit%20Declines/Best%20Fit%20or%20Refit%20a%20Decline%20from%20a%20Point.md) the group.
However, you must manually remove the summation data from **Predictions
\| Data** first.

If, after best fitting the a group, you want to return to the original
manual forecast, click ![](../../../Images/Edit-a-Group-Forecast-1.jpg) on **Predictions \| Declines**
and then click **Recalculate**. You can also click
Recalculate Groups in the
Entity menu. See
[Recalculate a Group Forecast](Recalculate%20a%20Group%20Forecast.md).

When you import new production, it is applied to the wells contained in
the group, not to the group itself. If you want the new production
included in the group, you must recalculate the group. See
[Recalculate a Group Forecast](Recalculate%20a%20Group%20Forecast.md).

To delete the manual group forecast and best fit the group

1.  Go to Predictions \| Data and delete
    the manual forecast (all the grey cells). Clicking the cell in the
    top left corner where the rows and columns intersect will select all
    cells in the grid.
2.  Go to Predictions \| Declines and
    click a product (in the product cards below the graphs) or go to the
    Predictions menu and click
    Best Fit.
