



# Delete a Group or Rollup

When you delete a group or a rollup, all the child entities are unlinked
from the group. Any data entered at the group level will be deleted with
the group; the changes are not transferred to the child wells. All edits
done to the individual wells are retained. If a deleted group consists
of wells that were taken from a rollup, the child wells are returned to
the rollup. If the rollup had been deleted before the group, the group’s
wells are returned only to the well list, the rollup is not recreated.

Deleting a group or rollup does not delete the child wells.

To delete a rollup

1.  Select the rollup in the Explorer.
2.  Right click the rollup and select **Delete Entity** from the menu
    that appears.
