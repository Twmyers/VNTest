



# Modify a Group or Rollup

To add a well to a group

1.  In the Explorer, select the entity you want to add to a group or
    rollup.
2.  Right-click the entity, point to Add
    to, and click **Group** or
    Rollup.
3.  In the **Add to Entity** dialog box, select the group or rollup and
    click **OK**.

If an entity you are adding to the group is already in another group or
common termination entity, a message will ask you if you want to remove
it in order to move it into the group. The well cannot exist in more
than one group or common termination entity.

To remove a well from a group or rollup

1.  In the Entity Explorer, expand the group or rollup you want to
    remove a well from.
2.  Click the well you want to remove to select it.
3.  Right-click in the Entity Explorer and select
    Remove from Group or
    Remove from Rollup.
4.  Click Yes to remove the well.
