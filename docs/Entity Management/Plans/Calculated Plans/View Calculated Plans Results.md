



# View Calculated Plans Results

You can view the results of calculated plans on the [Generate Reports on the Reports Tab](../../../Generate%20Reports/Reports%20Tab/Generate%20Reports%20on%20the%20Reports%20Tab.md), and in the
[Create and Run a Batch](../../../Generate%20Reports/Use%20the%20Batch%20Manager/Create%20and%20Run%20a%20Batch.md)

Calculated plans behave like plans when you run reports.
