



# Add a Product to an Entity

To add a product to an entity, you must add the product to a product
list first. You can then specify that the entity use that product list.

To add a product to an entity

1.  If required,
    [Create a Custom Product and Product List](Create%20a%20Custom%20Product%20and%20Product%20List.md).
2.  In the Entity Explorer, right-click the entity you want to add the
    product to and click **Edit Entity.**
3.  Select the **Product List** that contains the product you want to
    add and click **OK**.

The product will be displayed on the relevant tabs.



If you want to forecast a custom product, you must select **Allow
Decline** when you create the product.
