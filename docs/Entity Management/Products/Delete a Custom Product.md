



# Delete a Custom Product

You can only remove a custom product from your project if the product is
not being used in a Product List.

To remove a custom product from a product list

1.  In the **Tools** menu, point to **Global Project Data** and click
    **Products**.
2.  Click the **Product Lists** tab.
3.  In the list on the left, click the product list that contains the
    product you want to remove.
4.  Move the product from the **Selected** column to the **Available**
    column.
5.  Repeat steps for any other product lists the product belongs to.
6.  Click **OK**.

To delete a custom product from your project

1.  In the **Tools** menu, point to **Global Project Data** and click
    **Products**.
2.  In the list on the left, click the product you want to remove.
3.  Click **Remove** and **OK**.
