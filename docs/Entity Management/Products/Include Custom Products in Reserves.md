



# Include Custom Products in Reserves

Default products (such as oil, gas, and condensate) are automatically
included in reserves management. However, if you want to include
additional products (such as injection gas or injection oil) or custom
products you have created, you must add them to a
[Jurisdictions](../../Reserves%20Management/Jurisdictions/Jurisdictions.md)



To add a custom product to a jurisdiction



1.  In the **Tools** menu, point to **Global Project Data** and click
    **Jurisdictions**.
2.  In the jurisdictions list on the left, select the jurisdiction.
3.  In the **Additional Products in Reserve Reports** list (under
    **Posting/Balancing**), select the additional products and click
    **OK**.
