



# Products

By default,
Value
Navigator is configured to enable forecasting on 13 products,
such as oil, gas, water, C2, and NGLs. However, you can also create
custom products, assign them ratios, enable decline analysis, assign
them import codes, and include them in reserves, if required.

All products are assignable to product lists. There are three default
product lists (gas, oil, and condensate), but you can create custom
product lists and add your custom products to them. You then assign
product lists to entities. For example, in creating a new gas well, you
would specify the Gas product list, which contains products such as Gas,
Oil, Water, and C2.



When you create a custom product, you must assign it to a Product List
in order to add the custom product to an entity.
