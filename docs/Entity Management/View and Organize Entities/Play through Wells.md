



# Play Through Wells

The Auto-play feature enables you to automatically scroll through the
wells in the Entity Explorer while viewing data in any tab of the Main
window. The Auto-play feature is a convenient way to review forecasts,
costs, or other data for a project. The Auto-play toolbar is undockable
and can be dragged anywhere on your screen.

| Button |  Function |
|----|----|
| 

![](../../Images/Play-through-Wells-1.png)

 | **First well** \| **Previous well** \| **Play** \| **Next well** \| **Last well** |

As Value
Navigator plays through the wells in the list, the well displayed
in the Main window is highlighted in the Entity Explorer. While
Value
Navigator is scrolling through wells, you can:

- switch between the horizontal or vertical tabs
- click other wells in the Entity Explorer to move to a different
  location in the well list
- flag wells for review later



Do not use the auto-play feature on the Reports tab unless you have
slowed down the speed of the auto-play considerably, as
Value
Navigator may not have enough time to generate the reports. Auto
Play will work on the Data View grids, but is more useful in Entity
View.



You can adjust the Navigation toolbar play speed by going to User
Options. The play speed is specified in milliseconds. Increasing the
time decreases the play speed.

To adjust the play speed

1.  In the **Tools** menu, click **Options**.
2.  In the **Options** dialog box, click the **User Options** tab.
3.  Under **General**, adjust the **Graph Play Delay** value and click
    **OK**.
