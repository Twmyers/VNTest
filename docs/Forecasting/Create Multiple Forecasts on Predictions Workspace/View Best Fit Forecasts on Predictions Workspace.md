



# View Best Fit Forecasts on Predictions \| Workspace

The Best Fit function enables you to best fit wells and view the results
on **Predictions \| Workspace** where you can compare the results of
several forecasts with different fit options. From **Predictions \|
Workspace** you can then determine which forecast you want to apply to
the well.

To apply a Best Fit and view the results in the Decline Workspace

1.  Follow the [Best Fit or Refit Wells](../Create%20and%20Edit%20Declines/Best%20Fit%20or%20Refit%20Wells.md) procedure and select
    **Create in Workspace** under Fit Target.
2.  Repeat step 1 with different Best Fit options, if required, and
    assign the forecast(s) a new name.
3.  Go to **Predictions \| Workspace** to view the best fit declines you
    created.
4.  Under Display, select the forecast(s) you want to display and
    compare the forecasts you created.
5.  Apply a forecast to a reserves category and [Apply a Forecast to a Reserves Category and Plan](Apply%20a%20Forecast%20to%20a%20Reserves%20Category%20and%20Plan.md)
