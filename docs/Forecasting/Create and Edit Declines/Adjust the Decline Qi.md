



# Adjust the Decline Qi

You can adjust the decline directly on the graph or in the decline
parameters on **Predictions \| Declines**.

To adjust the Qi on a graph

1.  On **Predictions \| Declines**, right click the graph you want to
    edit, point to **Decline** type and click **Initial Rate**.\
    The mouse pointer turns into a hand.
2.  Use the hand to point to the Qi you want to use and left-click to
    set the Qi.

To adjust the Qi under Declines

1.  On **Predictions \| Declines** click in the Qi field under
    **Declines** and enter the new Qi.
2.  Press **Enter**.
