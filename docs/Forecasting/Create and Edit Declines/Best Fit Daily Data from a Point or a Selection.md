

# Best Fit Daily Data from a Point or a Selection

Fitting daily data from a point involves selecting a point in the
production history.
Value
Navigator determines the best decline based on the production
history from that point forward, ignoring the history before it.

Fitting daily data from a selection involves selecting a segment of
production history.
Value
Navigator determines the best decline based on that selection,
ignoring the history before or after it.

To fit daily data from a point or selection you can use the following
keyboard shortcuts.

- **Shift + Click** (fit from a point)
- **Ctrl + Shift + S** (fit from selection)

To best fit data from a point in production history

1.  On **Predictions \| Declines**, right-click the **Rate/Cum** graph.
2.  Point to **Oil Decline**, and click **Daily Fit Oil/Gas from Point**
    or **Daily Fit Oil/Gas from Selection**. Your mouse pointer turns
    into a hand.
3.  Do one of the following:
    | To | Do this |
    |----|----|
    | Fit from a point | Click a point on the history. |
    | Fit from selection | Hold the mouse button down, drag the selector across the history, and release the mouse button. |
