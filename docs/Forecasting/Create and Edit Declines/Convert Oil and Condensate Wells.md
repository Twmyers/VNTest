



# Convert Oil and Condensate Wells

The **Convert Oil/Condensate** tool converts oil forecasts to condensate
or condensate forecasts to oil.

To convert oil forecasts to condensate or condensate forecasts to oil

1.  Select the entity to be converted or filter to the entities to be
    converted.
2.  On the **Administration** menu, point to **Data Cleanup** and click
    **Convert Oil/Condensate**.
3.  Select **Convert Oil to Cond**. or **Convert Cond. to Oil**.
4.  If you want to convert the children of groups, select **Apply to
    children of groups**.
5.  Click **OK**.
