



# Enter and Edit Plant Data

Before entering plant data, you must have a Master Gas Analysis. If you
need to add a Master Gas Analysis, see
[Add a Gas Analysis](Add%20a%20Gas%20Analysis.md).

To enter or modify plant data such as Surface Loss, Process Loss, Fuel
Loss, Energy Content, Ratios, or Efficiencies

1.  Go to **Predictions \| Data \| Plant**.
2.  [Select an Input Mode on the Plant Tab](Select%20an%20Input%20Mode%20on%20the%20Plant%20Tab.md) (Raw,
    Inlet, or Sales).
3.  [Modify the Inlet or Outlet Gas Analysis on the Plant Tab](Modify%20the%20Inlet%20or%20Outlet%20Gas%20Analysis%20on%20the%20Plant%20Tab.md) if required.
4.  [Select Data to View on Predictions | Data | Plant](Select%20Data%20to%20View%20on%20Predictions%20Data%20Plant.md)
5.  Enter the
    [Enter Surface Loss](Enter%20Surface%20Loss.md), [Enter Process Loss or Fuel Loss](Enter%20Process%20Loss%20or%20Fuel%20Loss.md)
6.  [Enter Volumes, Rates, Ratios, or Effiiciencies on the Plant Tab](Enter%20Volumes,%20Rates,%20Ratios,%20or%20Effiiciencies%20on%20the%20Plant%20Tab.md) for each component.
