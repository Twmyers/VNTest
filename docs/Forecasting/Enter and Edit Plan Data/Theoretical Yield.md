



# Theoretical Yield

Theoretical Yield is displayed on **Predictions \| Data \| Plant**. It
is based on the Inlet Gas Analysis and is a plant-based value. There is
no Theoretical Yield in Sales mode because plant efficiencies are not
valid in this mode.

Theoretical Yield is expressed in terms of the gas stream you are
entering ratios on.

The theoretical yield is the amount of product that would be recovered
assuming a recovery factor of 100%.
Value
Navigator uses the following product factors when calculating
Theoretical Yield:

| Item          | Product Factor |
|---------------|----------------|
| Ethane (C2)   | 6.35           |
| Propane (C3)  | 6.54           |
| Butane (C4)   | 7.62           |
| Pentane (C5+) | 9.6            |

The product factors are based on industry standards. The formula for
Theoretical Yields is as follows:

| Raw Yield = %Vol * Product Factor * (1-Surface Loss) (Note that Yield is a plant-based value so yield expressed as raw gas needs to consider surface loss) Inlet Yield = %Vol * Product Factor Example Volume % for C2 = 7.27 (obtained from Master Gas Analysis) Product Factor for C2 = 6.35 Theoretical Yield = 7.27 x 6.35 = 46.1645 bbl/MMcf |
| --- |
