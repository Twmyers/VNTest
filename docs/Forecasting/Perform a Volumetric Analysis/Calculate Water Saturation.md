



# Calculate Water Saturation

You can calculate water saturation while doing a volumetric analysis on
**Predictions \| Volumetrics**.

To calculate water saturation

1.  On **Predictions \| Volumetrics**, click **Calc. Sw**.
2.  Select values for **a**, **m**, and **n** by selecting the
    appropriate lithology from the window on the right.
3.  Enter the connate water resistivity value in the **Rw** field
    (0\4).
4.  Enter the reservoir porosity (%) in the **Porosity** field (if it is
    not already populated from **Predictions \| Volumetrics**).
5.  Enter the formation resistivity in the **Rt** field (0\10 000).
6.  Click **Calculate Sw**.
7.  If you want to use the calculated Sw in your volumetric analysis,
    click **Use Calculated Sw**.\
    The **Sw** field on **Predictions \| Volumetrics** is populated with
    the calculated value.
