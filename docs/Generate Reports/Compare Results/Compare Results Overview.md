

# Compare Results Overview

On the **Comparison** tab, you can compare economic or technical data
between entities or within different plans or reserves categories in one
entity or folder.

On **Comparison \| Economic**, you can compare production, cash flow,
and costs in different archives, reserves plans, calculated plans,
reserves categories, and scenarios.

On Comparison \| Waterfall, you can
compare run a reconciliation on any well or folder and see the
differences against any other version of that well. You an compare
different reserves categories, plans, or archives to see how wells or
fields are changing over time and quickly re-sequence the calculation to
explore sensitivities.

On **Comparison \| Technical**, you can compare declines between
Reserves Categories and Plans within one entity.

For economic comparisons, keep in mind that:

- All results are based on economic calculations and require economics
  to be run.
- Only results after the [Economic Calculation Start Date](../../Options/Project%20Options/Project%20Options%20Economics.md#Economic_Calculation_Start_Date) are displayed.
- Results are reported on a monthly basis until the [Last Year for Economic Monthly Output](../../Options/Project%20Options/Project%20Options%20Economics.md#Last_Year_for_Monthly_Output). Then
  results are displayed annually.
- Production is based on the entity’s Economic Limit (if that option is
  enabled).

To compare reserves balances, see [Compare Reserves Balances on the Waterfall Chart](../../Reserves%20Management/Automated%20Reserves%20Categories/Compare%20Reserves%20Balanaces%20on%20the%20Waterfall%20Chart.md).
