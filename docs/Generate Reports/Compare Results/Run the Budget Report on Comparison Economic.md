



# Run the Budget Report on Comparison \| Economic

The Budget Reports display values for the entities or folders, archives,
plans, and reserves categories that are selected on **Comparison \|
Economic**, with detailed monthly and annual data and total and
cumulative graphs.

When you set an entity as the[Set a Variance Baseline for Remaining Gas or Oil on Predictions | Workspace](../../Forecasting/Create%20Multiple%20Forecasts%20on%20Predictions%20Workspace/Set%20a%20Variance%20Baseline%20for%20Remaining%20Gas%20or%20Oil%20on%20Predictions%20Workspace.md), the budget reports display the variances.

The following information is displayed for each of the comparison
selections:

| Selection | Description |
|----|----|
| Before Tax Cash Flow | Total, Cumulative, and Graphs of: Revenue, Royalties and Burdens, Operating Costs, Net Operating Income, BTCF. |
| Costs | Totals of all Capital Cost types are displayed, plus data and graphs of Total Capital and Cumulative Capital. |
| Production | Calendar rate and Total Production in BOE, plus Total Cumulative Production. |

To display the Budget Report on **Comparison \| Economic**, click **Show
Budget Report**.
