



# Change NPV Discount Rates in Reports

Several economic reports and reserves reports include the Net Present
Value at several discount rates or just one discount rate. The rates
displayed in the reports are specified in the Project Options.

To change the discount rates displayed in reports

1.  From the **Tools** menu, select **Options**.
2.  In the **Options** dialog box, select the **Project Options** tab
    and click **Economics** on the left.
3.  Edit the Discount Rates and click **OK**.



Reports with only one discount rate use the middle value in the Project
Options: Economics.
