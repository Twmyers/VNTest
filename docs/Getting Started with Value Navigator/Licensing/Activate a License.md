



# Activate a License

Each installation of
Value
Navigator requires license activation with a product key. When
you start Value
Navigator for the first time after installation, the License
Manager is displayed and you can enter the product key. If you don’t
know your product key, contact your administrator or contact
[Value Navigator Support](../../../Support.md).

If you have an internet connection, you can enter the product key and
activate your license. If you don’t have an internet connection, contact
for assistance.

To enter the product key

1.  From the **Help** menu, select **License Manager**.
2.  Click **Change Product Key**.
    | If… | Do this… |
    |----|----|
    | You are connected to the internet, | Enter the new product key. |
    | You aren’t connected to the internet, | Click **Phone** in the top right corner of the **License Manager** and phone [Value Navigator Support](../../../Support.md). |
3.  Click **OK** and **Close.**



Unless you have checked out a license, (see
[Work Offline](Work%20Offline.md)) you
must have an internet connection to acquire and retain a license each
time you use
Value
Navigator. However, there is some allowance for an intermittent
internet connection so you don’t lose your connection to the license due
to a brief disconnection.
