



# Price Files

The Price Deck is global and applies to all entities in the project.
Sales Prices, Royalty Prices, Inflation Rates, Tax Rates and Exchange
Rates are all available in the price deck. You can use an evaluator’s
price deck, or build your own.

Price Files for use in
Value
Navigator are posted on our website quarterly. We supply price
files from the following evaluators: Mc Daniel, Sproule, GLJ, Deloitte,
Trimble, and Ryder Scott. We also post a Flat price deck. The price
decks supplied by the evaluators are converted to \*.xml format.

To access the price files you need a user name and password to log into
the secure area of our website. To receive a user name and password,
please contact
[Value Navigator Support](../../../Support.md).

If you already have a user name and password, log into
[https://community.quorumsoftware.com](https://community.quorumsoftware.com/) and search for
Price Files.



Users of these price files are asked to recognize the high degree of
uncertainty associated with forecasting oil and gas prices. As we did
not create or verify the source data provided, we are not liable for any
errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in the numbers provided. In addition,
