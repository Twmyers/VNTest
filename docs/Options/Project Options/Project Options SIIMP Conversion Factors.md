



# Project Options: SI/IMP Conversion Factors

Default Metric/Imperial conversion factors used by
Value
Navigator. By default, the conversion factors are the
International standard conversion factors but can be modified to
accommodate the use of differing conditions of pressure and temperature
in different parts of the world.

## Metric to Imperial

If enabled, the units are converted from Metric units to Imperial units.
Liquid volume converts from m3 to bbl, and gas volume converts from
m3 to scf.

## Imperial to Metric

If enabled, the units are converted from Imperial units to Metric units.
Liquid Volume converts from bbl to m3 and the Gas Volume converts from
scf to m3.

## Liquid Volume

The Liquid Volume field displays the factor that is used to convert
liquids from one unit system to another.

## Gas Volume

The Gas Volume field displays the factor used to convert gas from one
unit system to another.
